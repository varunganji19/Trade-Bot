What gets checked

Make the result
answer harder questions.

  1. 01

    Does it survive uncertainty?

    Bootstrap intervals show the range around the result, including the pessimistic end.

  2. 02

    Does the edge survive its costs?

    Compare the same trades at half, normal and double fees. Missing fee data stays visible.

  3. 03

    Was it consistent across time?

    Look at chronological quarters and market regimes. A trade-list check cannot refit a strategy.

  4. 04

    How many chances did you give it?

    Trial-adjusted Sharpe and, where measurable, overfitting probability account for selection.

The workflow

1 / Bring the trades

A Freqtrade export or a CSV of closed trades. Supply fees, trials and regime prices when available.

2 / Read the checks

Get a report through the local command line or dashboard. Every missing check is named.

3 / Decide what to test next

Robust, fragile or likely overfit describes the evidence. It is never a promise of future returns.
