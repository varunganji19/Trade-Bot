What is stored

Submitting the waitlist form stores your email address, the submission and expiry times, this consent-notice version and a hash of a private deletion token on the server hosting this page. The address is not verified. There are no advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

What it is used for

The project owner can export the active waitlist to contact people about validation access or user research. This application does not send email, sell a mailing list, or upload signups to another service. Only register an address you control.

Expiry and deletion

A signup expires after 30 days and is excluded from exports. Expired rows are purged on the next signup, export or maintenance run. Save the private deletion link shown after submitting: it deletes that submission immediately. Repeat submissions have separate links.

Brief connection information

The application keeps small in-memory connection buckets for rate limiting. They are not part of the signup database. A hosting provider or reverse proxy may keep its own access logs; its operator must configure their retention. Deletion tokens stay in a link fragment and are sent only in a deletion request body.

Limits of this notice

This describes the implemented local service. Deployment identity, contact details and any additional hosting or processing services must be disclosed by the operator before a public launch. This notice is not a claim of legal approval.
