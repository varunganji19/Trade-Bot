How risk controls work
Daily kill switch (automatic): after a −3% day it blocks new entries for the rest of the UTC day. It resets by itself at the next UTC day. It never force-closes positions — their stops, targets and strategy exits keep running.
Pause trading (manual): stays until you press Resume. Blocks new entries only — open positions are still managed (stops, targets, strategy exits). Nothing is force-closed.

How ownership works

Each timeframe runs one specialized strategy: 1h → turtle_trend, 15m/5m → vwap_scalper, 4h/1d → connors_meanrev. 5m scalping is enabled by explicit decision but measured unprofitable (BACKTESTS.md) — pull it back by reverting the scalper's timeframes. One position per symbol across timeframes (risk rule). Max 12 specs.
